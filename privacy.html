Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy describes how the Courtside 60 Pipeline (the "App") handles data. The App is an internal publishing tool operated by the owner of the Courtside 60 YouTube channel. It creates and publishes tennis preview videos to that one channel.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API). By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data accessed through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Who the App is for

The App is used only by the channel owner to manage the Courtside 60 channel. It does not offer sign-in to the public and does not access any other user's Google or YouTube account.

What data the App accesses

With the channel owner's authorization (Google OAuth), the App accesses:

  • Courtside 60 channel and video data: the channel's videos, titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status, scheduled publish times and playlists, so it can upload and update the channel's own videos.
  • Courtside 60 analytics: aggregate, anonymous reporting such as views, watch time and audience retention for the channel's own videos, used to measure which formats work.
  • Public comments on Courtside 60 videos: read to flag comments that need a reply or moderation by the channel owner.

The App does not access, collect or store personal information about viewers beyond what YouTube makes available to the channel owner (for example, a commenter's public display name alongside a public comment).

How the data is used

Data is used only to publish and manage Courtside 60 videos and to produce performance reports for the channel owner. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to build profiles of individuals.

Sharing

Data accessed through YouTube API Services is not shared with any third party. Reports stay with the channel owner.

Storage and retention

The OAuth authorization token is stored encrypted at rest on the channel owner's own computer (and, if the pipeline is later moved, in the owner's private Google Cloud project). Analytics and comment snapshots used for reports are kept for at most 30 days and then deleted, and are refreshed from YouTube API Services rather than kept as a long-term copy.

Cookies and device information

The App is not a website or app used by the public. It does not place cookies or store or access information on viewers' devices. This website itself uses no cookies or analytics.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the App's access to their Google account at any time from the Google security settings page. Revoking access stops all uploads and data access immediately; stored data is then deleted within 7 days.
