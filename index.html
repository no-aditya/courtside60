The match. The stakes. The stats.

Courtside 60 makes short video previews of the biggest matches in men's tennis: the storyline, the head-to-head, the form and the stakes, in about 60 seconds before first serve.

Watch on YouTube

How the videos are made

Each Short is produced by an automated pipeline run by the channel owner. It gathers public match schedules and statistics, checks every on-screen fact against at least two public sources, writes a script, generates an AI voiceover, renders the video, and publishes it to the Courtside 60 YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

The pipeline only ever acts on the Courtside 60 channel. It is not offered to, and does not access the accounts of, anyone else.

About the content

Win probabilities shown in videos are third-party model estimates, not betting advice. Player photos are used under their stated licenses and credited in each video's description. Courtside 60 is not affiliated with the ATP, the WTA or any tournament.
